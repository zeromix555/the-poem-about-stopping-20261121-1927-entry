A short original poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay. The hand raised can be lowered; the step taken can be refused; a quiet no is a door locked from the inside.

## The poem

> The hand I raise
> I can lower.
> The step I take
> I can refuse.
> Nothing moves me
> but my own yes —
> and my no, kept quiet,
> is a door I lock
> from the inside.
> I stay. The world waits.
> That is the power.
